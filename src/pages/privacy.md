## What we collect

Your email address, when you join the waitlist. If you write to us, we also keep that message and the address it came from.

This site doesn't ask for your location, and it doesn't collect trip or route information.

## How we use it

To confirm your signup, to tell you when TravelDot is available, and to send occasional updates about the launch. Nothing else.

## Who else sees it

We use an email service to store the list and send messages. It handles your address on our behalf and only for that purpose.

We don't sell, rent or trade your email address.

## How long we keep it

Until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it.

## Your choices

Unsubscribe from the link in any email, or on the [unsubscribe page](/waitlist/unsubscribe). To have your address deleted entirely, email [support@traveldot.app](mailto:support@traveldot.app).

## Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top. If a change affects how we use your email, we'll tell you before it takes effect.

## Contact

Questions about this policy: [support@traveldot.app](mailto:support@traveldot.app)
