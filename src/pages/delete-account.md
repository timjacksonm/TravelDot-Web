TravelDot is an app by Northland Software LLC. You can delete your TravelDot account in the app or by email, and you don't need to reinstall the app to ask.

## In the app

1. Open the **Account** tab.
2. Tap **Delete account** and confirm.

We delete the account 30 days later. Until then you can still sign in, but you can't check trips, and **Keep my account** cancels the deletion. After the 30 days we erase your account, trip history, favorite trips, and preferences, and email you a confirmation.

## By email

Email [support@traveldot.app](mailto:support@traveldot.app) from the address on your account, with the subject "Delete my account". We schedule the deletion the same way, 30 days out. If you want it gone sooner, say so in your email, and we'll delete it as soon as we reasonably can once we confirm the account is yours.

## Cancel TravelDot Plus first

Deleting your account doesn't cancel a TravelDot Plus subscription, because Apple or Google bills it. Cancel it in your phone's subscription settings. On an iPhone, that's Settings, then your name, then Subscriptions. On Android, open the Google Play Store, tap your profile picture, then Payments and subscriptions.

## What we keep

A few records stay after your account is deleted: purchase records for refunds and taxes, server logs for 14 days, and copies of trip checks with nothing that identifies you. Our privacy policy lists each one and how long we keep it, under [Data retention and deletion](/privacy/#data-retention-and-deletion).
