## 3. Road and weather information

TravelDot shows road conditions, incidents, closures, and weather relevant to a trip. Google provides the route used to identify the roads and areas to check. Road reports come from public sources, including official transportation information published by the Minnesota Department of Transportation (MnDOT). Weather information comes from Vaisala Xweather, and trip summaries are generated by an AI model using available reports and weather information.

TravelDot presents road reports using the reporting agency's wording. It does not independently verify reports, rate road conditions, or determine whether a trip is safe.

Information may be delayed, incomplete, or inaccurate. Reports may arrive late or remain available after conditions change, and many county and city roads may not be covered. Weather information is based on forecasts and may not reflect actual conditions along your trip. AI-generated summaries may misstate, omit, or misinterpret information from the underlying reports.

When you open a trip in Google Maps or Apple Maps, that app independently plans a route between the same starting point and destination. It may choose different roads from those checked by TravelDot.

TravelDot is not an emergency service. Official road closures, posted signs, law enforcement directions, and emergency instructions take priority over information provided by TravelDot. In an emergency, call 911.
